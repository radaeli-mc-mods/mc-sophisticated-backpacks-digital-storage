<p align="center" style="text-align: center;">Connect your Sophisticated Backpack to <b>Applied Energistics 2, Refined Storage, Tom's Simple Storage, and Super Cool Calculator</b></p>

<p align="center" style="background-color: #F4ECD0; text-align: center;"><img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/radaelilucca/mc-sophisticated-backpacks-digital-storage/main/docs/gallery/section-titles/what_it_is.png" alt="WHAT IT IS?" width="267"></p>

Your backpack can do more than carry supplies. **Digital Storage Expansion** adds nine upgrades that connect Sophisticated Backpacks to digital storage networks, so you can send items away, restock, and craft with network supplies while you play.

Each upgrade links to one network endpoint and follows that network's access and range rules. The addon adds item and fluid transfers; it does not add a storage network of its own.

<p align="center" style="background-color: #F4ECD0; text-align: center;"><img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/radaelilucca/mc-sophisticated-backpacks-digital-storage/main/docs/gallery/section-titles/how-do-i-use-it.png" alt="HOW DO I USE IT?" width="371"></p>

1. Hold a Remote Upgrade and **Shift-right-click a compatible network block** to link it. For Applied Energistics 2, use a **Controller**, **Terminal**, or another online network device; cables are excluded, and an **ME Wireless Access Point** on that network must cover you. For Refined Storage or Super Cool Calculator, use a **Controller**, **Grid/Terminal**, or another online network device, excluding cables. Super Cool Calculator still requires a **Network Antenna** with valid coverage. For Tom's Simple Storage, use a **Storage/Crafting Terminal**, **Inventory Connector**, or another connected network device. Plain and framed cables are excluded; the network still needs an available terminal.
2. Watch the upgrade change: its screen turns dark and its icon takes the color of the network (purple for AE2, blue for Refined Storage, oak brown for Tom's Simple Storage, gold for SCC), and a short animation plays when you link or switch networks. The four corners always show which networks are supported.
3. Place the linked upgrade in a backpack upgrade slot and open its tab to configure filters, priorities, or transfer settings.
4. Craft Remote Upgrades with **3 diamonds**, **2 Ender Pearls**, **2 redstone dust**, and the matching advanced Sophisticated Backpacks upgrade. For **Remote Crafting**, use the regular **Crafting Upgrade**.
5. To change networks, link the upgrade to another compatible block. To remove its link while keeping its settings, place the upgrade by itself in a crafting grid.

<p align="center" style="text-align: center;"><img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/radaelilucca/mc-sophisticated-backpacks-digital-storage/main/docs/gallery/upgrade_link_states.png" alt="Remote Feeding Upgrade unlinked, then linked to Applied Energistics 2, Refined Storage, Tom's Simple Storage, and Super Cool Calculator" width="420"></p>

> **Heads up:** Remote access follows your network's range and access rules, including its wireless setup. **Remote Pump** supports fluids with Applied Energistics 2, Refined Storage, and Super Cool Calculator. Tom's Simple Storage is item-only.

These upgrades do not create wireless access or bypass your storage network's permissions and range.

<p align="center" style="background-color: #F4ECD0; text-align: center;"><img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/radaelilucca/mc-sophisticated-backpacks-digital-storage/main/docs/gallery/section-titles/requirements.png" alt="REQUIREMENTS" width="355"></p>

Install the mod on **both the client and server**.

- **Minecraft 26.1.2 · NeoForge 26.1.2.124+ · Java 25** — Sophisticated Backpacks 3.26.20+ and Sophisticated Core 1.5.34+.
- **Minecraft 1.21.1 · NeoForge 21.1.236+** — Sophisticated Backpacks 3.26.3+ and Sophisticated Core 1.5.1+.
- **Minecraft 1.20.1 · Forge 47.4.10+** — Sophisticated Backpacks 3.26.12+ and Sophisticated Core 1.5.10+.

For **26.1.2**, use **AE2 26.1.13-beta** with **GuideME 26.1.14-beta**, **Refined Storage 3.2.1**, **Tom's Storage 2.10.3**, or **SCC 1.6.5**.

On the earlier Minecraft versions, connect each upgrade to the network you use: **Applied Energistics 2** (19.2.18+ on NeoForge; 15.4.11 on Forge), **Refined Storage** (2.0.9+ on NeoForge; 1.12.4 on Forge), **Tom's Simple Storage** (2.4.2+ on NeoForge; 1.7.1 on Forge), or **Super Cool Calculator** (1.5.3+ on NeoForge 1.21.1). You can use more than one network; each upgrade keeps the network you link it to.

For full setup instructions, filter behavior, and upgrade details, see the [documentation](https://mods.radaeli.dev/en/mods/sophisticated-backpack-is-addon/): [linking](https://mods.radaeli.dev/en/mods/sophisticated-backpack-is-addon/player/linking), [filters and source priority](https://mods.radaeli.dev/en/mods/sophisticated-backpack-is-addon/player/filters), and the [upgrade catalog](https://mods.radaeli.dev/en/mods/sophisticated-backpack-is-addon/player/upgrades).
